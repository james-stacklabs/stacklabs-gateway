The Roadhouse Origin

WildSeed was born at a wooden bar counter in Oklahoma, not in a venture capital pitch deck. While multi-state corporate conglomerates raised millions to build sterile warehouses full of rockwool cubes and chemical salt feeds, Michael Mclaren recognized a simple truth:

Working people don’t want irradiated cardboard weed wrapped in three layers of corporate plastic. They want soul. They want real dirt.

WildSeed is anchored in Oklahoma living soil—a microbial sanctuary of mycorrhizal fungi, beneficial nematodes, and composted native mulch. No synthetic bottlenecks. No chemical flushes. Just pure resin heads washed in cold mountain water and pressed under low-temp hydraulic plates.
